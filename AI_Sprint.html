AI Sprint

For teams that want clarity before commitment: four focused sessions that establish where you stand, what AI can realistically do for you, and what it would take to get there.


  



What the AI Sprint is

The AI Sprint is a short, structured engagement — four sessions of ninety minutes — built to answer one question properly: where does AI actually belong in your organization, and where does it not?

It is deliberately small. You get a defensible answer and a plan you can act on, without committing to a long programme before you know what it should contain.

Current state analysis and needs assessment

We look at how you work now — processes, data, tools, and the decisions that matter — and establish where AI would make a real difference and where it would merely add cost.

Strategic implementation plan

We turn that assessment into a sequence: what to do, in what order, and what each step requires of you.

Deliverable: a comprehensive discovery report and a strategic implementation roadmap.


Where it sits

The AI Sprint is the middle of our three consulting formats. The AI Hackathon is a single intensive day on one specific challenge; the AI Marathon is the full transformation programme. Many organizations start here, then continue into the Marathon with a plan already in hand.


Not sure which format fits? Talk to us and we will tell you honestly.
